# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

WonderWoW is an educational project for children, and its community should be a
place where anyone can take part safely and comfortably. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with children who use
  the app.
- Respecting different viewpoints, experience levels and design opinions.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected and learning
  from the experience.
- Putting the well-being of the project and its young audience first.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Insulting, demeaning or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Publishing someone else's private information without explicit permission.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a space aimed at children.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions and commit messages, and also when an individual is representing the
project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports are reviewed as quickly as
possible and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, reject
contributions or block accounts that violate this code, and will explain the
reason for any action taken.
